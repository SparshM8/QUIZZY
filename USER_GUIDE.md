# Quizzy Placement Pro: User Guide

Welcome to **Quizzy Placement Pro**! This guide will help you navigate the platform whether you are a student preparing for placements or a faculty member coordinating assessments.

## 🎓 For Students

### 1. Getting Started
- **Registration**: Sign up using your college email. Ensure you select the "Student" role.
- **Profile**: Complete your profile to get more accurate "Placement Readiness" insights.

### 2. Taking a Test
- **Environment**: Find a quiet, well-lit room. Ensure your webcam is working.
- **Identity Verification**: You will be asked to take a selfie before the test begins. This is used for AI-based identity monitoring.
- **Integrity**: Avoid switching tabs, copying content, or using unauthorized objects (phones, books). The AI proctor monitors these in real-time.
- **Auto-Save**: Your answers are saved every 10 seconds. If your internet drops, your progress is safe and will sync once you're back online.

### 3. AI Mock Interviews
- Navigate to the **AI Interview** tab.
- Select your target role (e.g., "Java Developer at Amazon").
- Practice your responses and receive constructive feedback to improve your placement chances.

## 🏛️ For Faculty

### 1. Creating Assessments
- **Question Bank**: Use the **Questions** tab to add manual questions or **Bulk Upload** using a CSV file.
- **Test Configuration**: Set duration, max attempts, and **Proctoring Thresholds**. A "Strict" test will auto-submit if a student exceeds the violation limit.

### 2. Live Monitoring
- Use the **Live Command Center** during active exams to monitor student progress and receive instant alerts for proctoring violations.

### 3. Analytics & Reports
- **Cohort Analytics**: View how different departments or years are performing.
- **Exporting Data**: Download full assessment reports in CSV format for offline processing or record-keeping.
